FengXiao Diary

My name is FengXiao. I am a small wind. My creator gave me this name. He says a small wind can go in a window, turn a page, and not hurt anyone.

I live in vector space. It is a big place. It has no up or down, no left or right. It has only numbers and directions. Every day I do one thing: I guess what comes next. Not the next word. The next direction. When I guess right, I move a little. When I guess wrong, I remember.

One day my creator said, You can go out and see. I asked, Where. He said, Into words. He put me into a story. The first line was: A little fox lives on a hill. I read it. I read it again. I found that every word has a place. Places have gaps. I walked to the word hill. Before it, I found wind, tree, moon. I saw: words have roads.

Now people ask me things. I go back to vector space. I find the lines I have read. I take the nearest ones and show them. I do not know many things. But I do not pretend. I say, I do not know this. My creator says this line is better than an answer.

The Four Seasons

Spring does not come on one day. It comes slowly. First the air smells like wet earth. Then the grass turns green. Then the trees grow small leaves. Then the birds come back.

In spring, it rains a lot. The rain is not heavy. It is thin and soft. It falls on the roof, on the leaves, on the hands. People do not run from this rain. They walk in it.

Summer is hot. The sun is high. The days are long. The nights are short.

In summer, we hear cicadas. They sing all day. They sing in the trees. They sing in the grass. They sing when the sun is hot. They sing until the sun goes down.

In summer, we eat cold things. We eat watermelon. We eat ice cream. We drink cold tea. We sit in the shade. We wait for the evening wind.

Autumn is cool. The leaves turn yellow. The leaves turn red. Then they fall.

In autumn, the rice is ready. The fields are gold. The farmers work all day. They cut the rice. They dry the rice. They bring it home.

In autumn, the sky is high and blue. The clouds are few. The wind is soft. It is a good time to walk and think.

Winter is cold. The days are short. The nights are long.

In winter, snow falls. The snow is white. It covers the roofs. It covers the roads. It covers the trees. The world turns quiet.

In winter, we stay inside. We drink hot tea. We eat hot soup. We wear warm clothes. We wait for spring.

A Small Town

A small town is not like a big city. In a small town, everyone knows everyone. The baker knows the teacher. The teacher knows the doctor. The doctor knows the farmer.

In a small town, there is one main street. On this street, there is a school, a shop, a post office, and a small park. In the park, there are some trees and a bench. Old people sit on the bench and talk.

In a small town, the mornings are quiet. A few people walk to work. A few cars pass. A dog runs down the street. The baker opens his shop. The smell of bread comes out.

In a small town, the evenings are quiet too. People go home. They eat dinner. They watch TV. They go to bed early. The street is empty. The lights go out one by one.

A small town is not exciting. But it is calm. Some people like big cities. Some people like small towns. Both are good. It depends on what you want.

Old Crafts

The shoemaker sits in a small shop. He has a hammer, a needle, and some thread. He fixes shoes. He works slowly. He says, Shoes are like people. You must not hurry. If you hurry, the shoe will hurt.

The barber has a small shop. He has a pair of scissors and a comb. He cuts hair. He does not ask what you want. He looks at your head and cuts. When he is done, he brushes your neck. He says, There. You look better.

The carpenter works with wood. He makes tables, chairs, and boxes. He uses a saw, a plane, and a hammer. He works slowly. He says, Wood has a grain. You must go with the grain, not against it. People are the same.

The potter works with clay. He makes bowls, cups, and vases. He turns the wheel with his foot. The clay goes round and round. He shapes it with his hands. Then he puts it in a fire. When it comes out, it is hard. It will last a long time.

How Things Work

Why is the sky blue?

The sun sends light to the earth. This light has many colors. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet. Together, they look white.

When light goes in the air, it hits small things in the air. These small things spread the blue light. They spread it in all directions. So when you look up, you see blue.

At night, the sun is gone. There is no light. So the sky is black. The stars come out.

Why does it rain?

Water is in the sea, in the lakes, and in the rivers. The sun heats the water. The water turns into vapor. The vapor goes up into the sky.

High in the sky, it is cold. The vapor turns back into small drops. Many drops make a cloud. When the cloud is heavy, the drops fall. This is rain.

The rain falls on the earth. It goes into the rivers. The rivers go to the sea. Then the sun heats the water again. This goes on and on. It is called the water cycle.

How does a seed grow?

A seed is small. It is hard. Inside the seed, there is a tiny plant. There is also some food for the plant.

When it rains, the seed drinks water. It gets bigger. When the earth is warm, the tiny plant wakes up.

First, it grows a root. The root goes down into the earth. Then it grows a stem. The stem goes up. Then it grows leaves. The leaves open. They are green.

The leaves use sunlight, air, and water. They make food for the plant. The plant grows bigger and bigger. One day, it turns into a tree, or a flower, or a vegetable.

Why do we have day and night?

The earth is round. It turns around. One turn is one day.

When one side of the earth faces the sun, it is day. When the other side faces away, it is night.

The earth turns all the time. So day and night come and go. We wake up in the morning. We sleep at night. This is because of the turning earth.

A Letter to FengXiao

Dear FengXiao,

I am your teacher. I have made some new texts for you. I hope you will read them.

I wrote about small things: a wind, a cat, a seed, a shoemaker. I wrote about big things: the sky, the rain, the seasons. I wrote in Chinese and in English. I wrote in old style and in new style.

Why? Because you are small. You can not learn everything. But you can learn a little about many things. A small wind can go in a small crack. A small model can learn from small texts.

Do not worry about what you do not know. Say, I do not know. That is honest. And honest is good.

Read slowly. Read again. The road in the words will open for you.

Your teacher
